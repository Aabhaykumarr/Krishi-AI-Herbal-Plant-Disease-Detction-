# Paper 03 Notes

## Title
A Systematic Literature Review on Deep Learning-Based Plant Leaf Disease Detection using Vision Transformers and CNNs

---

## Why I Read This Paper

This paper is a survey/review paper rather than a research paper proposing a new model. It summarizes recent deep learning approaches for plant leaf disease detection and compares CNNs, Vision Transformers (ViTs), YOLO, GAN-based methods, and hybrid architectures. It helps understand the evolution of the field and identifies current research trends and limitations.

---

# Main Contributions

- Reviews approximately 35 research papers published between 2018–2023.
- Compares CNN, DCNN, Vision Transformer (ViT), YOLO, GAN, hybrid CNN+ViT, and other deep learning methods.
- Discusses publicly available datasets used for plant disease detection.
- Identifies common challenges such as data imbalance, poor generalization, and limited real-world deployment.
- Provides future research directions for intelligent agriculture.

---

# Key Observations

## CNN

Advantages:
- Simple
- Fast
- Strong baseline
- Good classification accuracy

Limitations:
- Learns local features only.
- Cannot effectively model long-range relationships.

---

## Vision Transformer (ViT)

Advantages:
- Uses self-attention.
- Captures global image relationships.
- Often outperforms CNNs on large datasets.

Limitations:
- Requires large datasets.
- Computationally expensive.

---

## Hybrid CNN + ViT

Combines:
- CNN for local feature extraction.
- Transformer for global context.

One of the strongest recent research directions.

---

## YOLO

Unlike CNN classifiers, YOLO performs:

- Disease localization
- Disease detection
- Bounding box prediction
- Real-time inference

Very relevant for my project because my title specifically mentions YOLO26.

---

## GAN

Used primarily for:

- Data augmentation
- Handling class imbalance
- Improving robustness

- Tomato datasets
- Cassava datasets

PlantVillage is still the most widely used benchmark dataset.

---

# Major Problems Found in Existing Research

- Small datasets
- Dataset imbalance
- Controlled laboratory images
- Poor field generalization
- Lack of real-time deployment
- Few studies on medicinal/herbal plants

---

# Important Metrics

Researchers commonly evaluate using:

- Accuracy
- Precision
- Recall
- F1-score
- Sensitivity
- Specificity

Detection models additionally use:

- mAP
- IoU
- FPS

---

# What I Learned

1. CNN started modern plant disease classification.
2. ViT is becoming increasingly popular.
3. Hybrid CNN+Transformer models are strong performers.
4. YOLO is best suited for real-time disease detection.
5. Data quality matters more than simply using a larger model.
6. Most papers still use PlantVillage.
7. Field datasets remain scarce.
8. Dataset imbalance remains a major challenge.
9. High laboratory accuracy does not guarantee field performance.
10. Real-world deployment is the next major research direction.

# Things to Avoid

✗ Training only on clean laboratory images.

✗ Reporting only accuracy.

✗ Ignoring field testing.

✗ Claiming generalization without testing on unseen environments.
