# Why was this paper written?

Deep learning models can detect plant diseases very accurately.

However...

Most of these models are "black boxes."

A farmer receives:

"Tomato → Early Blight"

But...

Why?

Which part of the leaf caused the prediction?

Can we trust the model?

This paper tries to answer those questions.

Its goal is not to propose another CNN.

Its goal is to review:

• Existing datasets
• Existing CNN methods
• Existing Transformer methods
• Existing traditional ML methods
• Explainable AI techniques
• Future research directions

# Explainable AI (XAI)

This is the biggest contribution of this paper.

Question:

Can AI explain WHY it predicted a disease?

Instead of only predicting

"Apple Scab"

the model should highlight

"These brown spots caused the prediction."

This increases trust.

Especially important in agriculture.

---

# What I Learned

A good plant disease system should not only predict correctly.

It should also explain

WHY

it made that prediction.

This is especially important if the system will be used by farmers.

For KrishiAI, incorporating XAI (such as Grad-CAM or attention visualization) would increase user trust and strengthen the research contribution.
